        Find care

        Start with an official directory, wherever you live.

        Your location is not detected or sent automatically. Open a directory to search your area there.

        Questions to ask a provider

        • Do you assess eating concerns for someone my age?
        • Do I need a referral, insurance, or proof of eligibility?
        • What would an appointment or tests cost? Is reduced-cost care available?
        • Can you help with nutrition, physical health, and emotional support together?
        A public hospital is not automatically free for everyone. Confirm fees, eligibility, opening hours, and available services directly.

        How information is used

        General education can help you prepare questions for a clinician. Individual assessment and recovery plans involve your care team.
