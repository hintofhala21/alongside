            Find care

            Find the psychiatric hospitals and hospital psychiatry services closest to you, or search by country and city.

            Alongside asks for your location only when you tap this button. An approximate position (about 1 km) is sent to Google Maps to find the closest hospitals. It isn’t saved.

            Ask a parent, carer or another trusted adult to help you find care near you. You can also look up hospitals by country and city below.

            Or search by country and city
            This search stays on this device until you choose a Google Maps search or open an external link.

            Questions to ask a provider

            • Do you assess eating concerns for someone my age?
            • Do I need a referral, insurance, or proof of eligibility?
            • What would an appointment or tests cost? Is reduced-cost care available?
            • Can you help with nutrition, physical health, and emotional support together?
            A public hospital is not automatically free for everyone. Confirm fees, eligibility, opening hours, and available services directly.
