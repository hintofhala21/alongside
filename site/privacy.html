Privacy and pilot limits

Alongside is a public pilot for exploring recovery support tools. Please use sample details while testing. The app provides support tools and an information library; it does not diagnose or provide clinical treatment.

Your browser notebook

Kids’ chat

This AI runs on your device and can make mistakes. Closing this chat clears it. Keep your name, address and phone number to yourself.

Your profile, preferences and check-ins are saved on this browser. These profile details and check-ins are not sent to an Alongside database or AI service. Live chat uses a separate connection only when you choose it. They are not encrypted by this app, and someone with access to this browser may be able to read them. There is no account sign-in or cross-device backup.

In Settings, you can delete and restart the local profile. Clearing browser data also removes it. This does not erase files you have downloaded or your browser history.

What is connected

Automatic WhatsApp sending is not connected. A saved phone number stays in this browser. Adults can choose live chat on the main chat page. Calendar exports are files you choose to download and import yourself. Guided prompts appear while Alongside is open.

GitHub serves this website and receives ordinary website requests, which may include technical information such as an IP address. Alongside includes no analytics or tracking scripts. Read GitHub's privacy statement for the hosting provider's practices.

Competition chat and shared demos

Library-mode conversations stay in this browser. In adult live chat, the messages you send go to Cloudflare and OpenAI. Recent turns are used for follow-up replies. Personal chats cannot be selected for the public judges’ page. In a connected Luna demo, the demo messages go to Cloudflare and OpenAI. Your profile, check-ins, location and contact details are not sent automatically. Use fictional scenarios; this is not a patient AI service.

Younger builders can run predefined fictional scenarios without sending personal typing. Free-text testing is for invited adult judges who confirm fictional use. An invitation code and a checkbox are not verified medical consent.

Personal and demo sessions expire after 24 hours and are cleaned up periodically. Browser history remains until cleared. Only a conversation reviewed and selected with the separate publisher code appears on the judges’ page. Anyone with its link can read that selected demo; it expires after seven days and can be unpublished sooner. Provider backups and logs have separate retention. OpenAI requests use store:false, which is not a Zero Data Retention arrangement.

External resources

Medical sources, care directories and therapy links open outside Alongside. Those services have their own privacy rules, eligibility and charges. A listing does not promise an appointment or free care. BetterHelp links are available only for visitors who select the adult age group.

Before patient use

The content still needs clinical review. Guardian verification is not connected. Younger visitors should explore with a parent or trusted adult. Do not use this pilot to share sensitive medical details or as a substitute for care.

For a medical emergency or immediate danger, contact local emergency services. Alongside has no live care team.
