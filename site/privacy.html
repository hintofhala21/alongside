Privacy and pilot limits

Alongside is a public pilot for exploring recovery support tools. Please use sample details while testing. The app provides support tools and an information library; it does not diagnose or provide clinical treatment.

Your browser notebook

Your profile, preferences and check-ins are saved on this browser. Profile details and check-in notes stay on this device. You can separately choose to share reminder times. Live chat uses a separate connection only when you choose it. They are not encrypted by this app, and someone with access to this browser may be able to read them. There is no account sign-in or cross-device backup.

The first-visit link uses a separate notebook for the current tab. Opening the shared link again starts an empty walkthrough. In Settings, you can delete and restart the local profile. Clearing browser data also removes it. This does not erase files you have downloaded or your browser history.

What is connected

If you connect WhatsApp, Alongside sends your number, time zone and reminder times to its private reminder service. You confirm from your own WhatsApp account. WhatsApp processes your number and the reminder. Reply STOP or use Reminders to turn off sending. When you agree to follow your plan, saved changes update your WhatsApp reminder times. The connection expires after 30 days unless you renew it.

If you connect a calendar, Alongside shares your chosen reminder types, times, time zone and discreet-title setting through a private calendar link. Anyone who has the link can read those events. Stop sharing in Reminders, then remove the subscribed calendar from your calendar app. Calendar apps choose when to refresh. A downloaded calendar file is a separate copy you manage in your calendar app.

GitHub serves this website and receives ordinary website requests, which may include technical information such as an IP address. Alongside includes no analytics or tracking scripts. Read GitHub's privacy statement for the hosting provider's practices.

Competition chat and shared demos

Breeze uses the age group you select in your profile. Every user can type or choose a suggested question. On younger profiles, the device recognizes supported general questions and sends only their prepared wording. Other drafts stay in the text box. Only the prepared wording, age group and language go to the AI service. Adults can agree to send their own messages through Cloudflare to OpenAI. Recent turns are used for follow-up replies. Personal chats cannot be selected for the public judges’ page. In a connected Breeze demo, the demo messages go to Cloudflare and OpenAI. Your profile, check-ins, location and contact details are not sent automatically. Use fictional scenarios; this is not a patient AI service.

Invited judges can also open a separate competition session with fictional scenarios. Only those sessions can be selected for sharing.

Personal and demo sessions expire after 24 hours and are cleaned up periodically. Browser history remains until cleared. Only a conversation reviewed and selected with the separate publisher code appears on the judges’ page. Anyone with its link can read that selected demo; it expires after seven days and can be unpublished sooner. Provider backups and logs have separate retention. OpenAI requests use store:false, which is not a Zero Data Retention arrangement.

External resources

Medical sources, care directories and therapy links open outside Alongside. Those services have their own privacy rules, eligibility and charges. A listing does not promise an appointment or free care. BetterHelp links are available only for visitors who select the adult age group.

Before patient use

The content still needs clinical review. Guardian verification is not connected. Younger visitors should explore with a parent or trusted adult. Do not use this pilot to share sensitive medical details or as a substitute for care.

For a medical emergency or immediate danger, contact local emergency services. Alongside has no live care team.
