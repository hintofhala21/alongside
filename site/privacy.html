Privacy

The app provides support tools and an information library; it does not diagnose or provide clinical treatment.

Your browser notebook

Your profile, preferences and check-ins are saved on this browser. Profile details and check-in notes stay on this device. You can separately choose to share reminder times. Live chat uses a separate connection only when you choose it. They are not encrypted by this app, and someone with access to this browser may be able to read them. There is no account sign-in or cross-device backup.

In Settings, you can delete and restart the local profile. Clearing browser data also removes it. This does not erase files you have downloaded or your browser history.

What is connected

If you connect WhatsApp, Alongside sends your number, time zone and reminder times to its private reminder service. You confirm from your own WhatsApp account. WhatsApp processes your number and the reminder. Reply STOP or use Reminders to turn off sending. When you agree to follow your plan, saved changes update your WhatsApp reminder times. The connection expires after 30 days unless you renew it.

If you connect a calendar, Alongside shares your chosen reminder types, times, time zone and discreet-title setting through a private calendar link. Anyone who has the link can read those events. Stop sharing in Reminders, then remove the subscribed calendar from your calendar app. Calendar apps choose when to refresh. When you run the Apple Calendar helper or shortcut, it supplies appointment start and end times for a check on your device. Your saved review contains the affected reminders and proposed times. A change you accept applies to the selected date. Appointment titles, locations and attendee names are excluded. A downloaded calendar file is a separate copy you manage in your calendar app.

GitHub serves this website and receives ordinary website requests, which may include technical information such as an IP address. Alongside includes no analytics or tracking scripts. Read GitHub's privacy statement for the hosting provider's practices.

Chat with Breeze

Breeze uses the age group you select in your profile. Pressing Send shares your typed message with OpenAI, together with your age group and language. The hosted site sends messages through Cloudflare. Recent turns are used for follow-up replies. Your profile, check-ins, location and contact details are not sent automatically. Chat sessions expire after 24 hours. Saved conversations remain in your browser until cleared. Provider backups and logs have separate retention.

External resources

Medical sources and care directories open outside Alongside. Each service sets its own privacy rules, eligibility and fees.

About this support

The content still needs clinical review. Guardian verification is not connected. Younger visitors should explore with a parent or trusted adult. Do not use this app to share sensitive medical details or as a substitute for care.

For a medical emergency or immediate danger, contact local emergency services. Alongside has no live care team.
