Who can see these conversations?

“On this device” reads this browser’s saved chats. It does not publish them. “For the judges” shows only Breeze demos explicitly selected with the publisher code, after a review confirming fictional details only. Anyone with the judges’ link can read those selected demos. They expire after seven days and can be unpublished earlier.

No profiles, check-ins, phone numbers or recovery details are included automatically. Live demos are for fictional competition testing, not patient care.
