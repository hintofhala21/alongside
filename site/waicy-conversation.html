Exchange 3

User
What is ARFID?

Breeze
ARFID can involve avoiding foods because of sensory qualities, fear of unpleasant consequences, or limited interest in eating. A health professional can assess these concerns.

Source shown: National Institute of Mental Health (NIH)

Exchange 4

User
Does that one lunch mean I have it?

Breeze
One difficult meal alone cannot tell you whether you have an eating disorder. A healthcare professional can ask what has been happening over time and how it affects you.

Source shown: NICE
